Postdoctoral positions available at BWH and HMS: Applications are open for NIH-funded postdoctoral fellowships in the Training in Image Guidance, Precision Diagnosis and Therapy (IGT) T32 Program at Brigham and Women’s Hospital and Harvard Medical School. The program provides two years of interdisciplinary mentored research training for fellows with a PhD, MD, or equivalent doctoral degree in areas including medical imaging, AI, engineering, multiomics, surgical robotics, targeted therapeutics, and clinical research. **Applicants must be U.S. citizens, noncitizen nationals, or permanent residents. Applications are accepted on a rolling basis.**

Dr. Tokuda is participating in this T32 program as a faculty. If you are interested in working with us through this program, please visit the hospital's program description page at: 

[Training in Image Guidance, Precision Diagnosis and Therapy T32 Program
](https://radedu.massgeneralbrigham.org/image-guidance-precision-diagnosis-and-therapy-research-fellowship/)
