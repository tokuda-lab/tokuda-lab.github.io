The 15th Interventional MRI Symposium ([imri2026.org](https://imri2026.org/)) concluded successfully in Boston, featuring 53 oral presentations, 95 poster presentations, 17 industry exhibits, and more than 200 participants.

On behalf of the Local Organizing Committee at [the National Center for Advanced Technologies for Image Guided Therapy (AT-NCIGT)](https://ncigt.org), we thank all presenters and coauthors for their outstanding contributions. We are also deeply grateful to our [exhibitors](https://imri2026.org/exhibitors/) for their generous support of the symposium and their long-standing commitment to advancing the field of interventional MRI. The symposium would not have been such a success without the dedication and support of our entire community.

Members of the Tokuda Lab played key roles in organizing the symposium. **Dr. Tokuda** led the symposium as Local Chair, alongside the General Chairs, Dr. Thomas Kahn (University of Leipzig), Dr. Jonathan Lewin (Emory University), and Dr. Clare Tempany-Afdhal (Brigham and Women's Hospital / Harvard Medical School), and the Local Co-Chair, Dr. Nobuhiko Hata. **Dr. Mariana Bernardes**, **Dr. Pedro Moreira**, and our colleague **Mr. Franklin King** worked tirelessly behind the scenes to manage the countless details and logistics that made the symposium possible. **Dr. Ruofeng Wei**, **Mr. Mario Fleury Rosa**, and **Mr. Rajdeep Banerjee** helped with operations during the symposium.

Thank you to everyone who joined us in Boston and helped make the 15th Interventional MRI Symposium such a memorable event. We look forward to seeing this community continue to grow and advance the field together.

The next Interventional MRI Symposium will be held in Leipzig, Germany on October 26-27, 2028. Please visit [https://www.imri2028.org](https://www.imri2028.org) for details.
